About ratings

MakanMates uses a 1–3 Worth Going score, not a 5-star Google clone.

  • 3 gold: strong recent TikTok interest, positive sentiment, excellent value or uniqueness.
  • 2 half-gold: good social interest, a niche pick, or a solid option in its area.
  • 1 grey: visit only if convenient — possibly viral, inconsistent, or overhyped.

TikTok is weighted first, Instagram second. Google Reviews are not the primary ranking signal. Viral and worth-it are shown separately. TikTok Heat blends recency and popularity so a place trending this week looks different from a long-standing classic. All social and promo figures in this build are demo data until a verified data service is connected.
